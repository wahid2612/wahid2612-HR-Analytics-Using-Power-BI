# 👩‍💼 Employee Attrition & Workforce Analysis using Power BI

This project presents a detailed analysis of employee data using **Power BI**, focusing on uncovering insights around employee attrition, job satisfaction, compensation, and engagement. The dataset includes demographic, job-related, and performance metrics for each employee, enabling both descriptive and predictive-level analysis.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

Each row in the dataset represents a unique employee, with columns capturing a mix of personal and professional attributes. The goal of the analysis is to identify patterns and key drivers of attrition, while also exploring trends related to employee satisfaction, compensation, and performance.

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### 👥 Employee Demographics
- Age, gender, marital status, education level
- Trends across different demographic groups and their correlation with attrition

### 🏢 Job-Related Features
- Job role, department, job level, job satisfaction, and involvement
- Comparison of satisfaction levels across roles and departments

### 💰 Compensation & Benefits
- Monthly income, hourly rate, stock options, and salary hikes
- Analysis of how compensation influences attrition and satisfaction

### ⚖️ Work-Life Balance & Environment
- Overtime patterns, environment satisfaction, and work-life balance
- Exploring how work culture affects retention

### 📈 Performance & Engagement
- Performance ratings, training frequency, and job involvement
- Measuring the relationship between engagement and job longevity

### 🕒 Experience & Tenure
- Years at company, in current role, since last promotion, and across companies
- Understanding tenure and growth within the organization

### ❗ Attrition Insights
- Identification of factors contributing to employee exits
- Used as the target variable for potential predictive modeling

---

## 🧠 Key Use Cases

- **Attrition Trends**: Identify which groups are more likely to leave
- **Satisfaction Drivers**: Explore factors influencing job satisfaction
- **Performance vs. Compensation**: Understand how pay and benefits relate to performance and retention

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI** for dashboard creation and visualization
- **DAX/Power Query** for data transformation and modeling

---

## 📌 Summary

This Power BI dashboard provides business stakeholders with a visual, data-driven understanding of workforce dynamics and attrition patterns, helping guide strategic HR decisions and retention efforts.
